All operational Kolkata Metro lines, suburban local corridors, and special midnight schedules for festival days (Saptami to Navami):

🚇 Blue Line (Dakshineswar - Kavi Subhash)

Festival Timings: 1:00 PM to 4:00 AM (Overnight)

Frequency: Every 6 to 8 minutes during peak hours, 15 mins post-midnight.

Key Hubs: Shyambazar, Esplanade, Kalighat, Rabindra Sarobar.

🚇 Green Line (Howrah Maidan - Esplanade & Sector V)

Festival Timings: 11:00 AM to 1:30 AM

Frequency: Every 10 minutes running smoothly under the Hooghly River.

Key Hubs: Howrah Station, Mahakaran, Sealdah, Salt Lake Stadium.

🚇 Purple Line (Joka - Majerhat)

Festival Timings: 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM

Frequency: Regular intervals connecting South-West suburbs to Behala.

🚇 Orange Line (Kavi Subhash - Hemanta Mukhopadhyay / Ruby)

Festival Timings: 8:30 AM to 8:30 PM

Frequency: Connecting Eastern Metropolitan Bypass corridors.

🚆 Suburban Local Trains (Sealdah & Howrah Divisions)

Special Midnight Locals: Operating late-night specials from Sealdah and Howrah terminals.

Destinations: Ranaghat, Naihati, Krishnanagar, Baruipur, Diamond Harbour, Bandel, Bardhaman, Kharagpur & Canning.

Extra security and unreserved ticket counters open 24/7.

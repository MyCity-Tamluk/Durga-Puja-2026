    📜 Maha Bhog & Traditional Recipe Corner

    🍲 Ashtami Bhoger Khichuri & Labra

    Key Ingredients: Gobindobhog Rice, Moong Dal, Potato, Pumpkin, Cauliflower, Ghee, Ginger paste, Cumin & Whole Garam Masala.

    Method: Dry roast moong dal till golden. Wash Gobindobhog rice. Sauté chopped vegetables in ghee with spices. Boil dal and rice together with vegetables until soft and aromatic. Temper with ghee, bay leaves, and dried red chillies.

    🍮 Traditional Narkel Naru (Coconut Sweet Balls)

    Key Ingredients: Fresh grated coconut, Jaggery (Nolen Gur or Date palm jaggery), Cardamom powder.

    Method: Mix grated coconut and jaggery in a pan on low flame. Stir continuously until the mixture thickens and binds together. Add cardamom powder, let it cool slightly, and shape into round balls with greased palms.
